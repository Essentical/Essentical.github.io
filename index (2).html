Approach

Three constraints decide everything.

Low cost

Built on a student budget from off-the-shelf parts. A design choice that makes the robot harder to afford needs a very good reason.

Sturdy

Made to survive everyday handling, not only a clean workbench. Simple and repairable beats clever and fragile.

Safe around people

The robot is meant to interact with people, so it has to be gentle and predictable. Safety comes before strength.

The first build is a humanoid, but the concept behind it is meant to carry over to any robot shape.

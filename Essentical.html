The Key

Your data. Gone if you forget the code.

The problem. Cloud storage is a liability. If the server is breached, your data goes with it. If the company changes its policy, your privacy goes too.

The solution. A hardware-encrypted drive that needs no software, no drivers and no internet. Type your PIN on the physical keypad and the drive unlocks. Pull it out and it locks.

Enter the wrong code three times and the encryption key is destroyed. The data is gone for good. We do not have the key. No one does.

The Switch

Physical truth. Digital silence.

The problem. Software can be hacked and firmware can be backdoored. A physical break in the circuit cannot be talked around.

The solution. A USB-C hub with a mechanical switch for every device you plug into it. Green means safe. Red means silent. No apps. No updates.

  • Mic killCuts power and data to a USB microphone
  • Cam killCuts power and data to a USB camera
  • Net killCuts a USB network adapter

The Switch controls what is connected through it. It does not disable a laptop’s built-in microphone, camera or Wi‑Fi.

  • SwitchesMechanical, rated for 100,000 cycles
  • IndicatorOne LED. Green or red.

What it protects. And what it does not.

We would rather you know the limits than guess them. A privacy tool you misunderstand is worse than none.

The Key

    Protects against

  • A lost or stolen drive
  • PIN guessing: three wrong codes destroy the key
  • A cloud breach, because your files are not in a cloud

    Does not protect against

  • A compromised computer while the drive is unlocked
  • Someone watching you type, or forcing you to unlock it
  • A forgotten PIN. Recovery is impossible.

The Switch

    Protects against

  • A USB microphone or camera listening or watching while switched off
  • A USB network adapter sending data while switched off

    Does not protect against

  • A laptop’s built-in microphone, camera or Wi‑Fi
  • Data that was already copied or sent
  • Anything that never used the connected devices

FAQ

Short answers. If yours is missing, write to us.

Is anything shipping yet?

Not yet. Join the waitlist and we will write once, when units are ready to ship.

What if I forget my Key PIN?

After three wrong codes the encryption key is destroyed and the data cannot be recovered. We do not hold a copy. Keep a backup of anything you cannot lose.

Does The Key need software or drivers?

No. You unlock it with the physical keypad, then use it like any USB-C drive.

Does The Switch disable my laptop’s built-in mic, camera or Wi‑Fi?

No. It cuts the devices connected through it, such as a USB microphone, camera or network adapter. Built-in hardware is not affected.

What does it not protect against?

Quite a lot, and we say so. See the limits.

Where is it made?

Each device is assembled by hand in Switzerland, then tested and sealed by a person.

Can it be repaired?

Yes. Both products are designed to be modular and repairable.

What do you know about me?

Only what we need to fulfil your order and what Swiss tax law requires us to keep. The devices send us nothing.
